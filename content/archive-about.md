**About the Archive**

Photos and video from Endless Library events. Anyone can browse; members can post.

**Posting guidelines**

- Tag every post with a year and an event.
- Start each post with a photo, even if it's mostly video. That photo is what shows in the gallery view.
- Wrap groups of photos in `[grid]` … `[/grid]` so they tile.
- Video: MP4 or WebM, 1080p, under 250MB. Please compress before uploading. A raw phone clip can be several gigabytes and is slow for everyone to load.
- Don't caption people with full names unless they've asked you to.

**Photos of you**

We take photos at our events and publish some of them here, where they're public and can be found by search engines.

If you'd rather not be photographed at an event, tell any volunteer on the day. No explanation needed.

If a photo of you is already here and you want it gone, email us at [ADDRESS] and we'll remove it. We won't ask why, and we aim to have it down within a couple of days.

Photos of children are only published with a parent or guardian's agreement, and never with names attached.
